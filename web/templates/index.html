What this is, and is not

  • Benchmark numbers are on Java (OWASP Benchmark) and are labelled preliminary until three triage runs finish.

  • LLM verdicts are advice, not proof. Do not paste secrets or code you may not share with a third-party AI API if you tick AI triage.
